# SheShapes AI brand system

## Character

Express human-centered AI, women-led technology communities, warmth,
collaboration, clarity, and confidence. Use minimal flat vector illustration,
rounded shapes, generous spacing, and one subtle technology motif per page.

## Palette

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Primary purple | `#7048C8` |
| Bright purple | `#C33EE3` |
| Lavender | `#B9BCEB` |
| Light lavender | `#E3D8F1` |
| Warm ivory | `#FAF8F1` |
| Soft blush | `#F8EEF0` |
| Body gray | `#4C4C4C` |
| Technology blue | `#42A8F5` |
| Cyan accent | `#62DCE8` |

Use purple as the primary signal. Keep blue and cyan as controlled accents.
Avoid black backgrounds.

## Logo

- Place a logo on every page, normally in the upper-right.
- Keep the logo within the safe margin and use a consistent width across the
  deck.
- Use `assets/logos/logo-white.png` on dark gradients.
- Use `assets/logos/logo-bright-purple.png` on light gradients.
- Do not recolor the light-page logo to magenta, pink, or the legacy brand
  color; its required value is `#C33EE3`.
- Never stretch, crop, rotate, outline, or add a drop shadow to the logo.

## Gradients

Use the nine full-page 1920×1080 SVGs in `assets/gradients/`.

- `01` and `09` are dark; pair them with the white logo and white primary text.
- `02` through `08` are light; pair them with the Bright Purple logo and Body
  Gray text.
- Use the supplied gradient as a background layer. Do not overlay another
  full-page gradient.

## Typography

- Chinese: Noto Sans SC or the closest available CJK sans serif.
- English body and labels: Inter or a clean sans serif.
- Decorative English titles: Cormorant Garamond, limited to covers and section
  dividers.
- Titles: 48–64 pt.
- Body: 22–28 pt.
- Captions: 16–18 pt.
- Body copy: `#4C4C4C` on light pages.

## Grid

- Canvas: 1920×1080.
- Safe margins: 96 px left/right and 72 px top/bottom.
- Default alignment: left.
- Center alignment: covers, section dividers, and activity instructions only.
- Default title width: no more than 60% of the page.

## Imagery

Prefer real people collaborating in natural light, with varied identities and
perspectives. Use replaceable Canva frames. Do not use generic humanoid robots
to represent AI.

Use no more than one decorative motif on a normal content page. The bundled SVG
library contains:

- 12 characters;
- 16 icons;
- 10 decorations;
- 6 presentation components.
